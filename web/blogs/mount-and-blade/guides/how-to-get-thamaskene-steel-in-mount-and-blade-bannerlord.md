The refining section of the smithy in Mount and Blade: Bannerlord is often overlooked. In this area, players can manipulate raw materials by combining or breaking them down to create new materials. 

Hardwood, for example, can be converted into charcoal, and many metals can be transformed into better versions of themselves. Players must first obtain the Steel Maker 3 perk, which allows them to refine two units of Fine Steel into one unit of Thamaskene Steel. This process can be repeated as long as there is enough charcoal and Fine Steel available. Reaching level 100 in the Smithing skill unlocks the Steel Maker 3 perk.
